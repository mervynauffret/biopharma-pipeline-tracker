Written for snapshot: 2026-11-19

## Chase the six silent milestones
Six trials have passed the sponsor's own estimated primary completion date and still show as running. The biggest is argenx's Phase 3 of empasiprubart versus IVIg in multifocal motor neuropathy: target Sep 2026, record last verified Apr 2026.
Ask: real delay or a record that hasn't been updated? Either way, the plan needs a new date.

## Watch single-asset concentration
23 of argenx's 42 active trials run on efgartigimod. Genmab's late stage leans on two oncology assets: Rina-S in 9 trials and epcoritamab in 13.
Ask: what is the portfolio's exposure if one asset reads out badly?

## Prepare for the readout window
Eight Phase 2+ trials expect primary completion by May 2027, including Pharming's paediatric APDS Phase 3 (30 Nov 2026) and Lundbeck's adolescent chronic migraine Phase 3 (26 Mar 2027).
Ask: are launch, supply and communication plans sequenced against these dates?
